# Draft hypotheses H1-H10 (written before the primary works were read; to be corrected by the synthesis and the attack lenses)

DRAFT MAPPING TO ATTACK (hypotheses H1-H10, written before reading the primary works):
H1 Loss/lifetime (review refs Woessner 2015 Nat. Mater. 14, 421; Ni 2018 Nature 557, 530; Dai 2015): at 1 THz and 300 K the acoustic-plasmon tau is set by the Drude momentum-relaxation time, tau = mu E_F/(e v_F^2) = 1.17 ps per 1e5 cm^2/Vs at E_F 117 meV. hBN-encapsulated exfoliated graphene reaches RT mobility ~1-1.5e5 at n ~1e12 (phonon-limited), so tau ~1.2-1.8 ps and Q ~7-11 are defensible, against Part I's best case tau = 1 ps (Q 6.3) and the measured baseline 0.4-0.6 ps. Effect: per-gate loss 13.65/Q dB falls from 2.17 dB (Q 6.3) to ~1.2-1.9 dB; this is the single material lever the review's loss literature supports, and it is inside Part I's existing parameter space, not a new mechanism. CVD material (mu ~1e4) stays overdamped (omega tau 0.73). hBN dielectric loss and gate-metal loss are second order at 1 THz.
H2 Confinement (Iranzo 2018 Science 360, 291; Epstein 2020; Alonso-Gonzalez 2017): tighter gaps (0.7-3 nm) do not improve any Fable number: the cell footprint is wiring-limited (1 um^2 active in 27.4 um^2), the 10-nm gap already gives q d = 0.027, and d < 10 nm raises nonlocal slowing, gate leakage and drive heat.
H3 Launch via graphene metasurfaces/antennas (Thongrattanasiri 2012 PRL 108, 047401 total absorption; Yao 2014 Nano Lett. 14, 6526 tunable perfect absorbers; Emani 2012; Dabidian 2015/2016; Papasimakis 2013; Ju 2011; Nikitin 2011): near-unity absorption into localized graphene plasmon resonances is demonstrated in the mid-IR and THz, but absorption into a localized resonance is not coupling into a propagating acoustic mode in a 10-nm gap; no free-space-to-acoustic-mode coupling efficiency at 1 THz exists; Yoshioka's optical launch was 0.005% vs 30-35% electrical. Bearing: design toolbox for Part I 9.5's least mature mitigation, not a quantified improvement.
H4 Electrical readout via plasmon-assisted photodetection (Yan 2012 Nat. Nanotechnol. 7, 330; Guo 2020 LSA 9, 29; plus Bandurin 2018 Nat. Commun. 9, 5392 resonant THz detection by graphene plasmons, Castilla 2019): these read plasmon energy as a DC photovoltage through photothermoelectric, bolometric or rectification effects with responsivities ~0.1-10 V/W (hmm, check) and NEP ~ nW/Hz^0.5-pW/Hz^0.5, but integrate over ns-us; Part III #141 already proposes a rectification read (+1.7-2.1% drain density per '1' within 6.9 ps). Bearing: evidence base for #141, no slot-rate (14.5 ps) readout demonstrated.
H5 Gain alternatives (Apalkov & Stockman 2013 LSA 3, e191 graphene nanospaser, mid-IR proposal; optically pumped graphene THz gain: Ryzhii 2007, Boubanga-Tombet 2012 PRL 108, 047401? (check), Watanabe 2013, Yadav/Otsuji 2018 'THz light-emitting graphene-channel transistor'): population-inversion gain in graphene is transient (ps), needs fs optical pumping or high current injection, and no CW THz plasmon amplifier exists; a spaser needs an external gain medium. Bearing: alternative route if G1 fails, not an improvement to the DS cell; energy per op would be set by the pump.
H6 Routing components (Ke 2015-2017; Liu 2016-2017; Huang 2017; Wang 2015 Opt. Express 23, 31136; Fei 2015 edge plasmons): simulated mid-IR graphene plasmonic waveguides, bends, Y-splitters and MZ modulators report insertion losses of order 0.5-3 dB per element; Fable budgets -1 to -3 dB per junction unrun. Bearing: design references for the Boltzmann-Maxwell tier and the F = 2 activation trees; no quantified improvement.
H7 Nonreciprocity/isolation (Poumirol 2013 PRL 110, 246803 and Yan 2012 Nano Lett. 12, 3766 magnetoplasmons; Stauber chiral plasmons in twisted bilayer; Hu 2017 PRL 119, 247402): Miller's isolation criterion is unmet by the released Fable cell, which relies on drift Fizeau drag (<= 3.6%) for routing sign; magnetoplasmons need tesla-class fields (impractical), chiral twisted-bilayer plasmons are theoretical. Bearing: not applicable at 300 K without magnets.
H8 Drude-weight scaling with stacked layers (Yan 2012 Nat. Nanotechnol. graphene/insulator stacks; Low 2014 ACS Nano bilayer plasmons): N decoupled layers multiply the Drude weight by N, lowering kinetic inductance and sheet impedance (72.8 ohm/sq per layer at tau = 1 ps), which could ease the stripline-to-plasmon impedance match and raise s by sqrt(N) for the gated mode; but it also lengthens lambda_p (footprint), raises the DS threshold drift (M = v0/s) and changes the gain geometry. Bearing: unknown/speculative; no Fable number changes without a new model.
H9 Hydrodynamic transport (Levitov & Falkovich 2016 Nat. Phys. 12, 672; Bandurin 2016; Polini 2016): confirms the e-e-collision-dominated regime at 300 K that Part I's DS hydrodynamics assumes (tau_ee ~0.1-0.2 ps, zero-to-first-sound crossover near 1 THz per Barcons Ruiz 2023). Bearing: already in record.
H10 Everything else in the review (SERS/GERS, sensing, Klein tunnelling, QHE, superconductivity in twisted bilayer, plasmon-phonon polaritons in hBN at 23-48 THz, graphene plasmon quantum nonlinearity at few-nm scale): not applicable to a 1-THz room-temperature logic fabric; Part II already places single-plasmon nonlinearity at ~3-nm, ~200-THz scales.
OVERALL DRAFT CONCLUSION: the review is a 2021 mid-IR-centred map of graphene plasmonics whose relevant primary works are already in Fable's reference base (Koppens, Jablan, Fei, Chen, Woessner, Ni, Iranzo, Alonso-Gonzalez, Ju); the only performance lever it supports is material quality (H1: tau from ~1 ps toward ~1.5-2 ps at 300 K), worth up to ~2x in Q and ~1 dB per gate, inside Part I's stated parameter space; the launch, readout and gain items are design toolboxes or alternative routes, not improvements; nothing in it bears on G1/DG1.
