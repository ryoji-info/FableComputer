OBSERVATIONS TO ATTACK (R1-R7, written by the session after reading the review):
R1 The only number in the review that bears on a Fable figure is the RT phonon-limited mobility 2.0e5 cm^2/Vs at 1e12. With tau = mu E_F/(e v_F^2) (E_F 117 meV, v_F 1e6 m/s): 2.0e5 -> tau = 2.33 ps -> Q = 14.7 at 300 K, per-gate loss 13.65/Q = 0.93 dB vs 2.17 dB at Q 6.3. Part I Sec. 4 already states tau = 1 ps <-> 86,000 cm^2/Vs, names the demonstrated hBN-encapsulated envelope ~140,000 (tau 1.63 ps, Q 10.2), gives the measured RT acoustic-plasmon baseline 0.4-0.6 ps, and says its single-rate kinetic operator restores a 353 K threshold only for a lifetime raised ~x2.6 (tau 2.6 ps <-> ~224,000 cm^2/Vs at 300 K). Acoustic-phonon-limited mobility scales ~1/T, so the intrinsic limit at 353 K is ~1.7e5 (tau ~2.0 ps). Conclusion: material quality can raise tau by x1.4 (demonstrated) to x2.3 (intrinsic) over the 1-ps row, inside the record's parameter space, but cannot reach the x2.6 the 353 K threshold needs in that operator; and measured plasmon lifetimes (0.4-1 ps) are far below either. The review adds no new lever here; it only restates the ceiling.
R2 The review's modelling default tau = 1e-13 s would make a 1-THz plasmon overdamped (Q = 0.63); its dispersion is the unscreened branch that the prior session showed to be a worse bit (lambda_p 19-24 um on sapphire, 54.6/Q dB per wavelength, DS threshold tau 6-15 ps). The gate-screened acoustic mode, drift gain and the 300-K THz operating regime are absent, so the review does not address Fable's bit at all.
R3 Multilayer supermodes ('loss decreases with the number of layers', Sec. 2.6) do not transfer to a gated cell: N decoupled sheets multiply the Drude weight by N, so the gated speed s and lambda_p scale as sqrt(N); loss per wavelength stays 27.3/Q (Q = omega tau unchanged) while loss per unit length falls by sqrt(N); the DS Mach threshold M_th = 1/(8 f tau) is unchanged but the threshold drift v0 = M s rises by sqrt(N), so Joule heat per area ~ N * n m* v0^2/tau rises ~N^2 against a thermal budget that already closes only for tau >= 0.92 ps at N = 1; the cell lengthens by sqrt(N). Bearing: no gain in loss per gate; worse heat; interlayer coupling and gate screening of lower layers unmodelled.
R4 Gain routes in the review — gapped-BLG resonant gain under population inversion (theory, mid-IR), the quantum-well nanospaser (proposal, mid-IR), the graphene-tuned THz QCL (3-4 THz, cryogenic) — are not applicable at 1 THz and 300 K: no QCL exists at 1 THz, THz QCLs run cryogenic or pulsed to ~250 K at ~4 THz, and population inversion in graphene lasts picoseconds under optical pumping. They are alternative routes if G1 fails, not improvements of the DS cell.
R5 Gate-defined plasmonic crystals (Xiong 2019: patterned dielectric + gate, LDOS x16, domain-wall modes, 60 K, mid-IR) and domain-wall plasmon reflection (Jiang 2016) are experimental support that patterned gates define plasmon propagation, bandgaps and reflecting walls — evidence for Part I's gate-defined junctions and CNP guard cells, owned by the unrun Boltzmann-Maxwell tier. Not a performance improvement; a reference for that tier.
R6 Twisted bilayer 'damping decreases with twist angle' (Hu 2017, mid-IR): check what was measured (plasmon wavelength and damping ratio vs angle; decoupled large-angle layers ~ doubled Drude weight). Even if real, it is R3's Drude-weight lever with the same heat penalty and no change in Q = omega tau unless tau itself rises. Photodetectors (Guo 2020 >40 GHz, 0.4 A/W at 1.55 um) and absorbers are edge O/E components; Fable's readout is electrical/photoconductive sampling; not applicable.
R7 The review's own admission that GSP metamaterial work is mainly theoretical/numerical because nanofabrication lags applies to Fable (nothing built); the review offers no fabrication route for 576-nm gate-defined cells with 10-nm hBN under Au at wafer scale.
R8 (from the loss_lifetime gather agent; verify) Part I Sec. 4's measured baseline 'tau ~ 0.4-0.6 ps, Q ~ 2.5-3.8 [39, 40]' takes Alonso-Gonzalez 2017's amplitude decay time L_PC/v_g (380-600 fs) as the Drude tau. For a Drude mode omega(omega + i/tau) = s^2 q^2 the amplitude decay time is 2 tau, so in the record's own convention the [39] baseline would be tau = 0.19-0.30 ps, Q = 1.2-1.9, and Part I's continuous-drive duty at the baseline would fall from 8-28% to 0.9-3.5%. Barcons Ruiz 2023 Fig. S7 gives momentum tau directly: 0.18-0.34 ps (11.8-nm hBN) and 0.32-0.48 ps (2-nm hBN) at 295 K, n ~ 1e12, under 2 nm Ti/15 nm Pd gates (which themselves add 14-27% damping by the image-current estimate). Check the convention in [39] (what the authors call tau_p and how L_PC is defined) and whether an ERRATA row is warranted (I-41 to I-43 do not cover it).
R9 (from the same agent; verify) Part I Sec. 4 reads Wang et al. 2013's '140,000 cm^2/Vs' as the demonstrated RT envelope 'near the acoustic-phonon limit'. Digitized Fig. 3B gives ~90,500 at n = 1e12 (tau 1.06 ps); the 140,000 peak is at n ~ 0.1-0.3e12 where tau is shorter (E_F ∝ sqrt n). Purdie 2018 gives 80,000 (electrons) / 103,000 (holes) at 1e12, 290 K. The DC envelope at 1e12 is therefore tau 0.93-1.20 ps and the acoustic-phonon ceiling 1.2-1.8 ps (Hwang-Das Sarma D = 19 eV: 1.02e5; Morozov rho_S <= 50 ohm: >= 1.25e5; Wang high-density data: >= 1.39-1.56e5; Chen 2008: 2e5). So R1's '2.33 ps, Q 14.7' is the optimistic end; the defensible ceiling band is Q 7.5-11.3 (per-gate 1.8-1.2 dB).
