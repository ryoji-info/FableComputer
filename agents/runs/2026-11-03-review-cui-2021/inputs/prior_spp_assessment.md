# Surface plasmon polaritons alone, or combined with the acoustic plasmon — assessment (2026-11-03)

Question (Ryoji, 2026-11-03): all simulations use the acoustic (gate-screened) graphene plasmon as the bit. What about a case using surface plasmon polaritons (SPPs) by themselves, or in combination with the acoustic plasmon (AP)?

Method: papers Part I v6.1 / II v2.1 / III v1.1 (repo copies, deposited 2026-10-29) grepped for the mode choice, the gate's roles and the launch chain; a 5-agent Opus workflow (wf_40e4178c-c81) sourced 135 items (metal SPPs at THz and optical frequencies; graphene plasmon branches and current-driven gain; hybrid optical I/O, photomixers, photonic-computing floors) and then attacked a six-claim draft with a physics lens (6 verdicts, 18 omissions) and a papers lens (18 verdicts, 22 omissions). Three sub-claims of the draft were refuted and are corrected below. Sourced items and verdicts: `spp_findings.txt` (same folder). Labels: 設計モデル / 公開モデル / 外部 / 導出 (derived here) / 未解決.

## 0. Bottom line

1. **SPPs by themselves cannot be the bit.** At 1 THz a metal SPP is a Sommerfeld–Zenneck wave: λ ≈ 300 µm (λ0 itself), field height ≈ 5 cm on smooth gold, no gate programmability, no drift gain. Spoof SPPs reach λ0/5 (λ0/10 near cutoff) with 37–125 µm features and no gain. At optical frequencies (1550 nm) the SPP is thermally dark (T_Q ≈ 9,280 K), which removes Part II's 48 K problem, but there is no electrically pumped plasmonic gain and no cascadable, level-restoring plasmonic logic family; every published SPP gate is a passive interferometer. That route is Part I Table 1's "photonic logic" row, a different project in which graphene appears only as modulator, absorber or detector material.
2. **The unscreened graphene plasmon (the "graphene SPP") is a worse bit than the acoustic one**, but for different reasons than the papers give. On the sapphire stack it has λp ≈ 19–24 µm (35 µm only in a uniform ε = 3.5 medium), so the cell is 8–15× longer and the lane 4–100× larger; it loses 54.6/Q dB per wavelength (twice the acoustic 27.3/Q) because its group velocity is half its phase velocity; and the Dyakonov–Shur threshold scales with mode speed, so at 1 THz it needs τ ≈ 6–15 ps (or a drift of 2.4–4.4 × 10⁶ m/s, above v_F and above v_sat). Gate programmability, however, is **not** lost: THz-transparent ZnO gates, ion gels and remote gates set the density without screening (Part I's own ref. [4] did exactly that).
3. **In combination, SPP or metal layers belong at the edges, where the design is already hybrid.** Three combinations have quantitative support: (a) a low-loss metal THz distribution layer (planar Goubau 1–3 dB/mm, CPW 6–9, thin-film microstrip 5–10) against ≈1,860 dB/mm for the acoustic plasmon at Q = 6.3; (b) an unscreened or THz-transparent-gated launch and transport section feeding metal-gated DS cells, because the only measured electrical-to-plasmon coupling (30–35 %, 4 K) belongs to the unscreened device, with the gold-gated acoustic device far lower; (c) plasmonic electro-optic modulators (≈1 THz bandwidth, 0.07–12 fJ/bit drive) for the operand lines. None of these changes the per-line THz launch energy, which dominates, and none bears on the gain gate G1 or the decision gate DG1.
4. **The dominant unbooked cost is unchanged by any SPP choice**: a 1-THz photomixed launch needs ≈32–39 mW of optical pump per line (P_THz ≈ 2.4 × 10⁻³ W⁻¹ · P_opt²), i.e. 0.13–0.57 pJ optical per launched '1' and 0.4–3.6 pJ electrical, or 0.3–1.4 pJ (optical) to 1–9 pJ (electrical) per MAC against the lane's 29–64 fJ/MAC and the ≈7 pJ/MAC HBM read. Electronic THz sources (RTDs, 0.03–2 % dc-to-THz) are one to three orders cheaper per THz joule but modulate at ≤30 GHz; Part III already lists them (#139) and an electrical write/read (#141).

## 1. Why the papers chose the gate-screened mode, and what the gate does

Part I Table 3 (the §4 operating point; not Table 1, which compares logic technologies) states: plasmon mode "acoustic / gate-screened (metal top gate) — required for confinement and gate programmability [4]". It gives no derivation; it cites Yoshioka et al. 2024 only. Part I §3.1 adds that in [4] the acoustic mode was obtained under a Ti/Au gate while "the ZnO-capped device hosted the unscreened mode", and keeps ZnO "as an option only where a THz-transparent global window is wanted". Part I §1 makes 1 THz a systems compromise: below ≈0.5 THz the quarter-wave cell outgrows the ≈500 nm pitch, "surrendering the footprint advantage that justifies plasmons in the first place"; above 1 THz the photomixer power and stripline bandwidth run out. Part I §10 calls the cell "strongly gated (q0d = 0.027) and quasistatic (ω/cq0 = 8 × 10⁻³)".

The papers lean on the metal gate for nine roles (papers lens Q2): confinement and the speed s(d) through Eq. (1); the per-segment voltage map that "is the program" (active cells, CNP guard cells); the DS source boundary condition ("AC-shorted gate-to-channel voltage at the source end", §9.1); gate-defined junctions (§9.4); the mandatory density and phase trims (+7 % to +48 % Δn/n; nm-class per-cell trim, §5, §9.7); logic-input coupling and the released cell's only inverter (an external gate swing, §5, §9.8); clock-gating of the re-sync stations (§9.6); charge memory ("the bit's persistent home is the charge on a cell's gate", §6.4); and the candidate electrical write (#141). The in-situ threshold calibration is a bias-current procedure, not a gate function.

Record points the lenses raised (not changed here; the repo is out of scope for this session):
- The Table 3 wording "required for … gate programmability" is too strong. Accurate: "required for confinement and for ≈1-V, sub-µm metal-gate programmability". Density can be set through THz-transparent resistive (ZnO; Tu et al. 2020 programmed cavity size, position and frequency this way, ≈90 % THz transmission at 1.3–10 THz) or ion-gel gates, or remotely (≈110–270 V at 6 µm).
- Part I §3.2/§9.5 say "no cited work quantifies the coupling efficiency", but its own ref. [4] reports ≈30 % (arXiv) to 35 % (journal) electrical-pulse-to-plasmon conversion, tied to the ZnO (unscreened) device; the Au-gated acoustic device is far lower (≈3 % appears only in secondary text, low confidence). Measured at 4 K per Part I Table 2.
- Part I §4's one sentence on gate-metal ohmic loss cites [41] (Woessner 2015), a mid-IR, back-gated, hBN/graphene/hBN-on-SiO2 study with no metal gate; it does not support the sentence. Derived here: Q_metal = ωL_k/R_s ≈ 1.6 × 10³ for bulk gold at 1 THz and n = 10¹² cm⁻² (L_k = 7.28 × 10⁻¹¹ H/sq, ωL_k = 458 Ω, R_s = 0.287 Ω), adding ≈0.4 % to the damping at τ = 1 ps, ≈2 % for 30-nm Au, but ≈23 % for a 15-nm Pd gate (as in Part I ref. [10]). Part I specifies no gate thickness or resistivity.
- The acoustic baseline itself is not yet demonstrated at room temperature in scalable material: De Fazio et al. 2026 (Part III ref. [43]) saw acoustic-plasmon cavity resonances in CVD graphene (Q ≈ 0.8–2.0 at 1.8–2.5 THz) only from 6 to 90 K, gone by 130 K; at μ ≈ 10⁴ cm²/Vs the 1-THz mode is overdamped (ωτ ≈ 0.73, derived).

## 3. SPP by itself

### 3a. THz metal SPP: no path to a bit
Gold at 1 THz has ω/ω_p ≈ 4.6 × 10⁻⁴ (ω_p = 7.28 × 10⁴ cm⁻¹ = 2,183 THz, Ordal 1985), |ε| ≈ 7 × 10⁵, surface impedance 0.287 − 0.335i Ω, skin depth 74 nm, reflectance 99.7 %. The surface wave is Re(n_SPP) − 1 ≈ 1.05 × 10⁻⁷, 1/e field height 5.4 cm (Nazarov 2008 Eq. 6: 5.8 cm), propagation 35 m. The metal contributes no plasmonic response at 1 THz, so "polariton" adds nothing and the wave cannot be confined, programmed or amplified by drift. Spoof SPPs (Pendry 2004: ω_pl = πc/(a√ε_h)) need 37–125 µm features near 1 THz and confine to ≈λ0/5–λ0/10 only near cutoff, where loss and dispersion rise. The only role for metal THz guides is transport (section 5c).

### 3b. Optical metal SPP: thermally dark, but no gain and no restoring logic
- Quantum regime: 0.7999 eV per photon, T_Q = 9,282 K, n̄ = 3.7 × 10⁻¹⁴ at 300 K. Part II's central cost (n̄ = 5.76 thermal quanta at 1 THz) disappears. In principle a shot-noise-limited analog machine works at 300 K (Hamerly et al. 2019). In practice receivers are Johnson/TIA-noise-limited unless detector capacitance ≲ 1.2–24 fF; Netcast's amplified receivers were thermal-noise-limited at 10–100 fJ/MAC; a demonstrated 3D-integrated 10 Gb/s receiver needs −24.85 dBm ≈ 330 aJ ≈ 2,600 photons per bit.
- Per-decision field energy (corrected arithmetic): 10–100 photons at 1550 nm = 1.3–12.8 aJ (0.128 aJ per photon; the draft's 3–30 aJ was wrong), against the THz cell's ≥406 quanta × 0.66 zJ = 0.27 aJ at 300 K (Part I §9.5). The THz penalty is in quanta, not joules.
- Gain: propagating-SPP gain has been shown only with optical pumping (8.55 dB/mm, De Leon & Berini 2010). No room-temperature electrically pumped net-gain SPP amplifier was found (Azzam et al. 2020; search to 2026-11-03). Spaser thresholds 1–100 MW/cm² at RT.
- Logic: Wei 2011 (Ag-nanowire NOR), Fu 2012 (24 dB contrast slot-waveguide gates), Birr 2015 (DLSPP half adder), Ota 2016 (4.3–6.8 dB on/off) are all passive linear interferometers: no fan-out, no level restoration, phase-critical biasing (Miller 2010 criteria). The closest room-temperature cascadable optical logic with gain is exciton-polariton (Sannikov 2024, −17/−18 dB, ≈4 pJ control pulses), not plasmonic. Restoration in an optical-carrier machine would come from SOAs or detector–transistor–modulator hybrids, i.e. electronics.
- Speed/energy: plasmonic EO modulators reach a 997-GHz 3-dB bandwidth (Horst et al., Optica 2025; 10–15 µm phase shifters), 0.07–2 fJ/bit drive (Heni 2019, modulator load only) to 12 fJ/bit (ring, 2.5 dB loss), with 2.5–15 dB insertion loss paid in laser power. System energy is set by E/O/E conversion and lasers (fJ to pJ per bit); photonic accelerators sit at 0.42 pJ/op including lasers (Lightelligence PACE) to ≈1.2 pJ/op (Lightmatter, Nature 2025), at 7.6–9 bits precision (>10 bits "difficult", McMahon 2023). SPP loss (0.1–3 dB/µm) makes an SPP carrier a worse host than dielectric photonics even here.
- In-model symmetry (papers lens): the released Fable cell also lacks restoration (Part I §5: no threshold AND, two '1's only 2.6–3.3 dB above one; no density-route NOT; the interference inverter gives 10 dB only within 10.1–12.3°). Its distinction from passive plasmonic logic is gain for fan-out, which is unproven (G1, G4).
- Part II positions itself against photonic accelerators on three claimed differentiators: ≈100× sub-free-space confinement, gate-programmable weights and routing, and ADC-free quantization by regenerative cells, conceding "the cost is the THz quantum". The ADC-free decoder needs k ≥ 16 comparators; the released cell's density coupling gives k = 0.18–0.26 (Part I §5). Part II never considers an optical carrier; it cites metal/optical quantum plasmonics (Altewischer 2002, Tame 2013) only as background and as an unassumed upgrade path.

### 3c. Unscreened graphene branch: numbers
λu = 35.3 µm × 7/(ε1+ε2) (Koppens 2011; E_F 117 meV, 1 THz). Sapphire (ε ≈ 9.4) plus air or a thin cap gives 19–24 µm; v_p ≈ 1.9–3.5 × 10⁷ m/s. Screening by a metal sets in at d ≈ λu/4π ≈ 2.8 µm (λ/λu = 0.74), 0.51 at 1 µm, 0.18 at 100 nm, 0.059 at 10 nm (local Drude with the Principi 2011 form; Lundeberg 2017 capacitance criterion). Lane area: Part III charges 27.4 µm² per cell of which 1 µm² is active (3.6 % fill). Scaling only the active area ×67–229 gives 93–255 µm² per cell, ×3.4–9.3 per lane (0.8–2.1 mm² instead of 0.22–0.23); scaling the whole footprint gives 15–53 mm². Either way the footprint rationale of Part I §1 is forfeited. DS: Part I Eq. (3) gives M_th = 1/(8f0τ) = 0.125 at τ = 1 ps; the threshold drift scales with mode speed, 0.29 × 10⁶ m/s gated vs 2.4–4.4 × 10⁶ m/s unscreened, above v_F (10⁶) and 1.7–3× v_sat(10¹²) ≈ 1.4 × 10⁶ m/s; Part I §7.1–7.2's own Pass 1–2 logic (drift route fails when v_sat/v_p is small; here 0.04–0.07) excludes it. Ballistic Cherenkov emission is impossible (Svintsov 2019: v_p ≥ v_F > u0). The only room-temperature current-driven THz amplification (Boubanga-Tombet 2020: ≈9 %, 0.37 dB, CNP-referenced, gated dual-grating-gate hBN/graphene/hBN, μ > 3 × 10⁴) is attributed by its authors to a non-DS mechanism. Mid-IR drift experiments show Fizeau drag (≤3.6 %) and extra damping, not gain.

## 4. Combining SPP/metal/photonic layers with acoustic-plasmon logic

The design is already photonic-plasmonic (Part I §9.8 "the integration … is the proposal"; §6.4 "a hybrid by design"): SiN microcomb → injection-locked DFB pair (one IQ-modulated) → UTC-PD → coplanar-stripline photoconductive launcher (Kusyak [7], −3 dB at 440 GHz; 1-THz extension untested) → acoustic mode; comb-fed optical taps drive the re-sync stations every 10–13 cells. Part III calls the ≈800 operand lines "harder engineering than the lane" and does not model them (limitation 12). Any SPP or photonic layer extends these two existing edge layers.

### 4a. Screened/unscreened hybrid (the one combination the record quantitatively motivates)
Yoshioka 2024 measured 30–35 % electrical-pulse-to-plasmon conversion with a THz-transparent ZnO gate (unscreened mode) and far less under Au (acoustic mode), at 4 K; optical launch reached 0.005 % (NTT release). At ≈3 % acoustic-mode coupling, a 10 aJ/4 ps launch needs ≈83 µW at the launcher, above every CW photodiode near 1 THz (≤24 µW at 0.914 THz; 2.3–10.9 µW at 1.0–1.04 THz). So: launch into a ZnO-gated or weakly screened section (coupling ≈10× higher, loss per µm 4–8× lower), then compress into the 10-nm-gated mode through a tapered gate distance (Part I §9.5 already names "tapered-gate impedance transformers"; λ/λu = 0.74 → 0.51 → 0.18 → 0.059 at d = 2.8 µm, 1 µm, 100 nm, 10 nm). Unquantified; the transition is loss of the class Part I §9.4 already budgets at −1 to −3 dB per junction ("mode mismatch through the ungated sliver"). What the unscreened section cannot supply: confinement and the gate-referenced AC short the DS boundary condition needs, so cells stay metal-gated. 未解決 (Boltzmann–Maxwell tier, bench).

### 4b. Metal THz distribution instead of plasmonic buffer trees
Part III §4.6 [D4] shares activations through F = 2 plasmonic buffer trees of 256·(2L − 2) cells (3,072 cells at 7 lanes). Metal THz guides lose 1–10 dB/mm (planar Goubau 1.1–3.4; CPW 5.9–8.6; thin-film microstrip 5–10; spoof 0.75 dB/mm at 0.3 THz) against ≈1,860 dB/mm for the acoustic plasmon at Q 6.3: two to three orders less per mm. Edge-to-centre paths of 0.6–1.7 mm (Part III's ≤1.7 mm² up to the extrapolated 12 mm²) would cost 1–17 dB on such lines, currently unbooked. The limit is again the stripline-to-plasmon transducer. Neither the papers nor the draft considered this.

### 4c. Plasmonic or photonic operand I/O
- Fit is not a plasmonic advantage. Si microring/microdisk modulators are 3.5–12 µm (533 Tx/mm² demonstrated in 3D integration; 112–200 Gb/s per λ in CPO), so 384–704 launch lines (Part III's 2–7 lanes) or 1,344–3,392 (the 17–49-lane extrapolation of the frontier assessment) need 0.7–6.4 mm². Only mm-long Si MZMs fail. Plasmonic modulators add bandwidth (to ≈1 THz) and thermal robustness (racetrack at 85 °C, 28× better than Si rings) at 2.5–15 dB insertion loss, and through P_THz ∝ P_opt² each optical dB costs ≈2 dB of THz.
- Per-line launch (the dominant cost, unchanged by the modulator choice): UTC-PD near 1 THz, P_THz ≈ 2.4 × 10⁻³ W⁻¹ · P_opt² (24 µW at 100 mW, 0.914 THz). Part I's 10 aJ per 4 ps (2.5 µW) needs ≈32 mW; Part III's duty-0.8 12-ps gated '1' (31–44 aJ, ×3.4–4.9) needs 33–39 mW. 10 mW gives only 0.24 µW (≈1 aJ per 4 ps). Energies: 0.13–0.57 pJ optical per launched '1' (gated vs CW), 0.4–3.6 pJ electrical at 16–30 % laser wall-plug. Launched bits per MAC = 2 + 8/L (Part III §8.1(b)): 3.14–6.00 at 2–7 lanes, 2.16–2.47 at 17–49 lanes (the readout 13/32 = 0.41 bits/MAC are detections, not launches; the frontier assessment's 2.6–2.9 counted both). Per MAC: 0.3–1.4 pJ optical (4–49× the lane's 29–64 fJ), 1–9 pJ electrical (15–300×); context: HBM read ≈ 0.18 J/token ÷ 25.6 G MAC ≈ 7.1 pJ/MAC. Totals at 7 lanes: 704 lines × 33–39 mW = 23–27 W optical, ≈145–170 W electrical at 16 %, against 0.5–0.6 W for the fabric at 300 K. Still excluded: comb-line amplification (comb lines carry 10–50 µW; 33–39 mW per line needs per-line amplification or injection-locked lasers), modulator loss, on-chip THz distribution, the transducer, and the DG4 requirement that every line be 27.9–32.7 dB quiet (σ_rel ≤ 2.3–4.0 %).
- Electronic alternatives the record already lists: RTDs ≈20 µW near 1 THz single-element, 0.73 mW from an 89-element array, to 1.98 THz; 2 % dc-to-THz at 764 GHz, 73 µW at 863 GHz; ≤30 GHz direct modulation, per-device PLL locking. DC energy per 10 aJ of THz: 0.5 fJ (2 %) to 32 fJ (0.031 %), against ≈625 fJ for CW photomixing (10⁻⁴ × 16 %). Pulsed plasmonic photoconductive emitters: 0.8 mW at 1 THz from 150 mW at 2 % duty. Part III #139 (source class open) and #141 (electrical write by gate depletion, read by the cell's own rectification; gate RC unmodelled).
- Electro-optic readout (a natural SPP hybrid, undemonstrated with graphene plasmons): a ≈1-THz-bandwidth plasmonic modulator could map the on-chip THz voltage onto an optical carrier. It cannot lift Part II's warm 1-bit limit, because the up-converted signal carries the 1-THz mode's own 5.76 thermal quanta.

### 4d. Carrier-frequency lever inside graphene (physics lens, unverified)
Graphene plasmons at 10–20 THz, below hBN's lower Reststrahlen band (22.8 THz), have n̄ = 0.25–0.04 at 300 K; at fixed τ, Q = ωτ rises ∝ f and the DS increment ≈ v0/L rises ∝ f; the gated quarter wave shrinks to 58–29 nm (below the ≈500 nm pitch Part I assumes). No sources or launchers exist there, and the photomixer/stripline chain that fixes 1 THz runs out above it. Listed as an alternative to moving to optical carriers, not as a recommendation.

## 5. What this does to the gates

- Net room-temperature gain in a gated cell is Part I's G1 (≥ +2.6 dB per pulse at ≤80 °C, Table 7), preceded by the open question whether a 353 K threshold exists at all (§10), and followed by G4 (two cascaded cells, "the single most informative outcome"). Part III's DG1 is the loaded half-adder decision error (≤6.3 × 10⁻⁹ per decision, fan-out 2, reported with the input-referred swing). No booking passes warm at the 99–277 quanta a loaded F = 2 cascade delivers (Part III abstract). The earlier messages in this session used "DG1" loosely for the gain question; the gain gate is G1.
- No SPP, unscreened or photonic choice changes G1 or DG1. The unscreened branch removes DS gain outright (threshold τ 6–15 ps). The choice does act on DG4 (launch jitter 28–33 dB quiet on 384–704 lines) and on the unquantified launch coupling, which is where 4a–4c live.
- If G1 or DG1 fails, the asset lost is drift gain in a gated cell. The remaining fields are photonic/plasmonic computing and electronic THz gain (InP HEMT amplifier 9 dB at 1 THz, Mei 2015; RTDs), both already in Part I Table 1.
- hBN phonon polaritons (22.8–24.7 and 40.8–48.3 THz; resonator Q to 283) play no role at 1 THz (Part I §1 agrees); current-driven graphene emits them only as spontaneous electroluminescence (Guo 2025, Abou-Hamdan 2025), no gain.
