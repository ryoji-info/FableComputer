FABLE COMPUTER CONTEXT (papers Part I v6.1, Part II v2.1, Part III v1.1; text extracts you may read: papers_txt/Part-I.txt, Part-II.txt, Part-III.txt (generate them in the run bundle with inputs/extract_papers.py); repo copies under papers/ — READ ONLY, never modify anything outside the run bundle directory).
Design point: bit = gate-screened (acoustic) graphene plasmon in a 576-nm quarter-wave cell; hBN-encapsulated graphene, 10 nm hBN to a metal (Au) top gate, on sapphire; n = 1e12 cm^-2, E_F = 117 meV, 1 THz carrier; s = 2.33e6 m/s, lambda_p = 2.33 um (lambda0/129), quasistatic (q d = 0.027). Gain = Dyakonov-Shur (DS) drift instability of the gated channel biased at 0.7 x threshold; the increment ~ v0/L; Part I Eq.(3) M_th = 1/(8 f0 tau) = 0.125 at tau = 1 ps. Q = omega*tau: best case tau = 1 ps -> Q 6.3 at 300 K (5.3 at 353 K); measured RT acoustic-plasmon baseline tau 0.4-0.6 ps (Q 2.5-3.8). Passive loss 27.3/Q dB per plasmon wavelength (4.3 dB per lambda_p at Q 6.3; per lambda_p/2 gate 2.17 dB at 300 K, 2.56 dB at 353 K). Gate-metal ohmic loss unquantified (derived elsewhere: ~0.4% of damping for bulk Au, ~23% for 15-nm Pd). Released cell in-model compresses rather than thresholds (no threshold AND: two '1's only 2.6-3.3 dB above one; no NOT except interference inverter 10 dB within 10.1-12.3 deg). Fan-out 2 closes at ~69 GHz in-model. Part III lane: 32 MACs per 14.5 ps slot (68.97 GHz) = 2.207 TMAC/s; 8,169-8,455 cells per lane + 256(2L-2) activation buffer tree cells; 27.4 um^2 per cell of which 1 um^2 active (3.6% fill); 7.78-10.22 uW per cell at 300 K; 29-39 fJ/MAC (300 K), 59-64 fJ/MAC (353 K); 64 weight + 13 readout lines per lane + 256 shared activation lines at 69 Gb/s (384-704 launch lines for 2-7 lanes), not modelled ('harder engineering than the lane', limitation 12). Launch chain: SiN microcomb -> DFB pair (one IQ-modulated) -> UTC-PD (2.9-10.9 uW at ~1 THz; 24 uW at 0.914 THz; ~10 aJ per 4-ps slot at 3 uW; P_THz ~ 2.4e-3 W^-1 P_opt^2) -> coplanar-stripline photoconductive launcher (-3 dB at 440 GHz) -> acoustic mode; transducer efficiency unquantified at 300 K (Yoshioka 2024 measured 30-35% electrical-pulse-to-plasmon at 4 K with a THz-transparent ZnO gate, i.e. unscreened mode; far lower under Au). Readout: photoconductive sampling; Part III #141 proposes electrical write by gate depletion (-0.17..-0.28 n for 10 dB) and read by the cell's own rectification (+1.7-2.1% drain density per '1', 90% in 6.9 ps); gate RC unmodelled. Launch must be 27.9-32.7 dB quiet (DG4: sigma_rel <= 2.3-4.0% on all lines). Decision: homodyne-limited decision at 6.3e-9 needs >= 406 quanta at 300 K vs 99-277 delivered by a loaded F = 2 cascade (no booking passes warm). Part II: 1-THz quantum 4.14 meV, T_Q 48 K, 5.76 thermal quanta at 300 K; 1-bit analog decisions at RT (3.9e-3 per shot), 0.6 effective bits per MAC. Gates: Part I G1 = single-cell per-pulse net gain >= +2.6 dB at <= 80 C (prerequisite; whether a 353 K threshold exists is open); G4 = two-cell cascade; Part III DG1 = loaded half-adder decision error <= 6.3e-9 with input-referred swing; DG4 = launcher jitter. Mitigations named for launch (Part I 9.5): photoconductive launch (4 K), tapered-gate impedance transformers, resonant antenna-coupled gate segment (least mature). Junction loss budget -1 to -3 dB per junction incl. mode mismatch through the ungated sliver (Part I 9.4), owned by an unrun Boltzmann-Maxwell tier. 1 THz is a systems compromise (Part I 1): below ~0.5 THz the quarter-wave cell outgrows the ~500 nm pitch; above 1 THz photomixer power and stripline bandwidth run out. hBN Reststrahlen bands (22.8-24.7, 40.8-48.3 THz) play no role at 1 THz. Everything in the record is in-model; nothing is built or measured; CMOS wins every axis except clock rate.
