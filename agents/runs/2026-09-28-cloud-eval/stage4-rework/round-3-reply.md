## 6. Limitations and open items

1. **The coupling is an in-model construct, and its scale is its stake.** Ideal-buffer density coupling: cell 1's drain kept AC-open (|r| = 1) and read non-invasively, κ applied as a buffer gain, no back-action. A physical junction cannot present an AC-open drain to cell 1 and an AC-short source to cell 2 at the same node, and a transmitting junction loads the drain: in the released loop model a lossless −1 dB junction (|r| = 0.454, the largest reflection a passive junction allows) takes cell 1's DS loop from 0.945 to 0.429 and the delivered drive from +16 to +2.6 dB, and Part I §9.4's own junction (|r| < 0.151) takes them to 0.143 and +0.04 dB (item 2, in-model; open-end reflection phase assumed, both biases printed, 08-12's `regen.loop_gain` disagreement noted). Every G4 and cell-to-cell number here is conditional on the unloaded regime; the loaded regime was not run in the time domain — a partially reflecting drain at |r| = 0.454 and 0.151 in the released solver is the one experiment that decides which regime item 2 calls open (🌊 Kinetic's round-2 seat) — and belongs, with back-action and #124's unpromoted supermode question, to the Boltzmann–Maxwell tier (Part I §9.4). Open.
2. **Planes.** The drain velocity is not an independent coupling plane at a current-clamped drain (u − u₀ = −M·(h − 1) to 7 %, item 8); the passive drain velocity is an O(Δx) residue (halving 240 → 480). A junction that mixes planes (density ↔ velocity) would sit between the density row and its −M-scaled copy, 18.4 dB apart at this bias — not 44 dB. Open.
3. **Bias fork.** Every row is at the ratio bias; the analytic-bias leg (07-31's) is not re-run. G4 is a ratio of two gains at one bias; 08-01 ratio-bias measured +0.4 to +1.2 dB on the single-cell gain between conventions, so a G4 transfer to the analytic leg is in-model and open.
4. **Grid, scheme and cfl.** G4 falls 0.05–0.08 per doubling and the passive transfer rises 0.4 dB; first-order convergence is assumed, not established (08-02 §4 measured sub-first-order floors, fitted p = 0.55–0.89; 08-06's fitted order straddles 1), and the N = 720 rung was run only at −6 / −3 dB on m = 30. 08-13's ghost-centre clamp-placement term — the released scheme's first-order boundary error, measured on the phase leg — acts on **both** coupled clamps here (cell 1's drain and cell 2's source), and its size on a two-cell chain is not separated from the grid ladder. **cfl (Table 8):** at fixed N = 240, cfl 0.2 / 0.4 / 0.8 moves G4_A(J = −1) to 1.774 / 1.581 / 1.485, the stage-2 peak floor to -8.35 / -4.70 / -3.37 dB, the slot-mode floor to -11.65 / -6.26 / -3.49 dB, cell 1's own floor to -16.69 / -11.83 / -8.89 dB and the residue to -8.11 / -5.70 / -4.21 dB (bias held at the cfl-0.4 value).
