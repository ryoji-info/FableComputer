A. HOW IT WAS COMPUTED

Source: only BLIND_SPEC_T1.md. All code is mine, in rebuild_t1y . Internal units: V, mA, ps, fF, kohm, nH.

Unknowns per pair (11): supply node v_S; per half (+ and -): junction voltage v_j, conduction current i_x, pad voltage v_P, winding current i_L, stub voltage v_st. Layout (r) has 583 unknowns, layout (c) 220.

Equations per half, with i_rho = (v_S - v_j - v_P - e_rho)/rho:
- C_b dv_S/dt = (v_clk - v_S + e_b)/R_b - i_rho(+) - i_rho(-)
- C_d dv_j/dt = i_rho - i_x - i_sh
- tau_d di_x/dt = frac I_full(v_j) - i_x
- C_p dv_P/dt = i_rho - i_L - (v_P - v_st + e_k)/R_k
- C_s dv_st/dt = (v_P - v_st + e_k)/R_k + I_drive
- L di_L/dt = v_P - R_L i_L - e_L, for all windings at once. L is one matrix: per pair L_h = L_odd/(1+k) = 4.2344 pH on the diagonal, -k L_h between the two halves; per link 0.5 M on (b+,a+) and (b-,a-), -0.5 M on (b+,a-) and (b-,a+), symmetric. M = -0.06 sqrt(L_odd,a L_odd,b) for a normal link, +0.06 for a reversed one. The square root is my choice for spread draws; it equals -0.06 L_odd without spread.
- v_clk = V_s win(t - dT/4)(1 + p cos(4 pi f_d t + pi(q + 1.5 d))). win as specified (3.0 ps raised-cosine edges centred at 0 and 0.75 T).
- Signal: v_odd = (v_st(+) - v_st(-))/2.

Integrator: BDF2 at a fixed step, linear part implicit through a precomputed inverse of (I - (2/3) dt A). The diode term is solved by three fixed-point iterations per step from an extrapolated predictor. Going from 3 to 6 iterations changed phases by 2e-5 degrees and levels by 2e-5 mV.

Time step: dt = 0.0145 ps (64 steps per carrier period, 1000 per clock period). Halving check, nominal point, no noise, all pair-slots 1 to 31:
- Layout (r): phase change at most 0.54 degrees (mean 0.43), level change at most 1.06 mV (0.38 %).
- Layout (c): phase change at most 0.54 degrees (mean 0.42), level change at most 1.03 mV (0.37 %).

Noise. Every resistor gets a series voltage source and each diode a parallel current source. Each source is held constant over one step, as an independent Gaussian number:
- Resistor R (ohm): sigma_e^2 = f x 2 k_B T R / dt [V^2, dt in s]. That is the two-sided density 2kTR, the same as the one-sided 4kTR. At T = 353 K and dt = 0.0145 ps: rho 2.84 mV, R_L 2.50 mV, R_b 1.30 mV, R_k 0.82 mV.
- Shot: sigma_i^2 = f x q |i_x| / dt [A^2]. This is the two-sided density q i_x, the same as the one-sided 2 q i_x. It is 0.235 mA at i_x = 5 mA.
- f = 1 in all noisy runs.
- Check: one pair with zero supply. The odd-mode power below 5 THz was 3.84e-7 V^2 at dt and 3.65e-7 V^2 at dt/2. It is dt-independent in band and below kT/2C (7.0e-7 V^2), as expected with the noiseless diode leakage term. The raw stub-node variance depends on dt, because R_k C_s = 1.5e-4 ps puts that noise far above any band of interest.

Drive: +-0.4 mA cos(2 pi f_d t) into the stub nodes, switched on abruptly for t in [sT - T/4, sT + 3T/4). I take "until the pair switches off" as the centre of the falling edge.

Starting state: everything zero at t = -4 ps. Each pair's supply is held at 0 until its own slot-0 rising edge, so no pair starts in a fictitious slot -1. For task 1 the pair starts from its DC point (unique root of v_j = V_s - (2R_b + rho + R_L) frac I_full(v_j); 0.774 V at 0.94 V) plus a 1 mV odd seed. The seed sets v_P odd = 1 mV cos(phi), v_j odd opposite, and i_L odd = (1 mV / Z) sin(phi), with Z = sqrt(L_odd/(C_p + C_s)).

Read window: starts T/4 + 0.25 ps = 3.875 ps into the pair's own slot and lasts two carrier periods (1.859 ps). X = 2 mean(v_odd exp(-i 2 pi f_d t)) over the samples in it. Level = |X| in mV, the amplitude of the carrier component. Phase = arg X against cos(2 pi f_d t).

Axis and sign. Axis_0 is the mean phase of the driven pairs for bit 1, about 7 to 11 degrees depending on V_s. A 4-pair row without noise showed that one link turns the phase by +134 to +135 degrees per clock depth (the pump-step half of 270 degrees). That is how "copies" looks on the carrier. So axis_d = axis_0 + 135 d degrees, and a pair's bit is the sign of Re(X exp(-i axis_d)). It follows that 4 depths flip the label. The expected logic treats the X3 to C links into the next slot as inverted, which is what the cross-wired loop of layout (c) needs. Expected values come from propagating majority logic over the links of the layout.

Wrong decision: |phase(run) - phase(run without noise and spread, same V_s and p)| > 90 degrees, in slots 1 to 31.

Spread draws: g ~ N(0,1) per pair from numpy default_rng, seed 20261123 for (r) and 20261124 for (c). Draw k always uses row k, at every V_s point. Noise seeds are 777000 + k for (r) and 778000 + k for (c).

C. WHAT I DID NOT EXPECT FROM THE SPECIFICATION'S DESCRIPTION

1. The nominal supply of 0.94 V lies below the pair's free-running threshold of 0.97 V; the pair oscillates there only with the pump. The levels 1.000 and 1.015 V lie inside the free-running range (0.97 to 1.14 V). The adder still computes correctly at both without noise and spread. All wrong decisions I found are at 1.015 V and are driven by the inductance spread.
2. At 1.015 V the failures in (r) sit only in the deepest stage (M3, G3, K3, S3, C4). There they come with collapsed levels of 10 to 65 mV in the read window. In (c), Fc fails on its own with the spread alone; it has one input and drives nothing.
3. In (c), one carry error in draw 3 at 1.015 V runs round the loop and spoils four consecutive sums (slots 18 to 21) before it clears.
4. At 0.895 V nothing goes wrong, but the S pairs are still small at read time: median 100 mV, down to 2.7 mV under noise. A "right" phase at a few mV is a thin margin that the 90-degree rule does not show.
5. A forward link turns the carrier phase by +135 degrees per clock depth. So "copies the sign" holds on axes that rotate with depth, and four depths invert the label. The spec does not say this; it is consistent with the cross-wired loop of (c).

D. WHAT I DID NOT RUN AND LIMITS

- Layout (a) was not run. I ran only a 4-pair row, to fix the axis convention.
- No scan of noise factor f; every noisy run uses f = 1 with spread together. The noise-only and spread-only splits were run only for (r) draws 1 and 12 and (c) draw 3, at 1.015 V.
- Each draw is one noise realisation; 32 draws per point. Zero errors in 52,576 decisions bounds the rate only to about 6e-5 at 95 %. The error counts at 1.015 V come from 2 runs, or 1 run, and are not stable statistics.
- The time-step halving was done only at 0.94 V, once per layout. Read-window placement and length were not varied.
- In task 1 I did not test large seeds below 0.97 V for hysteresis. I did not check whether the low-level S pairs at 0.895 V grow later in the slot.
- The drive switches abruptly; I did not test a smoothed drive.
- Without spread, M_link = -0.06 L_odd exactly. With spread I used the geometric mean of the two pairs' L_odd, which is my choice.
