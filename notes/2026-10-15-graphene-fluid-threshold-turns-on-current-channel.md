### 3. The derivation, and what the sources say

**The fluid.** One Dirac band (v = v_F p̂, degeneracy 4; holes neglected, n_h/n_e = 0.0045 at 353 K), lab density n_op, gate potential energy per electron (e²/C)·δn with e²n_op/(C·E_F) = g/2 (Eq. (1)'s g = 9.34357). The drifting base state is the boosted Fermi–Dirac gas f₀ = F((p − u p_x − μ)/t); its lab-frame densities and fluxes are, exactly (v_F = 1), n = γn_r, J = nu, P = γ²Wu, E = γ²W − p, Π_xx = γ²Wu² + p, Q = P, with W = ε + p = 3p (conformal) and n_r, p the rest-frame Fermi–Dirac values at the rest-frame temperature t_r = γt. Conservation:

  ∂ₜn + ∂ₓJ = 0;  ∂ₜP + ∂ₓΠ + (e²/C)n∂ₓn = F₀n − P/τ;  ∂ₜE + ∂ₓQ + (e²/C)J∂ₓn = F₀J,

with F₀ = P₀/(n₀τ) the DC force that holds the base drift against friction. The friction −P/τ is what elastic, isotropic, energy-independent momentum relaxation gives exactly (kinetically −(δf − ⟨δf⟩_θ)/τ); it removes momentum, not energy, so the drive's work F₀J heats the electrons. Closures: **energy-conserving** (energy equation kept; τ_E → ∞), **iso** (comoving temperature t_r held; τ_E → 0), **isolab** (the boost-form t held — Svintsov 2013's Eq. (1) at fixed T). A friction family 1/τ ∝ n_r^b (b = +½ acoustic phonons / short-range, b = −½ charged impurities, b = 0 the released law) is run in all three.

**The cavity and its eigencondition at finite u.** Linearizing about the uniform drifting state with e^{−iωt} gives A·y′ = (iω·∂U/∂w + ∂S/∂w)·y over the cell. With the record's clamps — δn(0) = 0 at the source, δJ(L) = 0 at the drain — and only the two plasmon legs carrying the solution, number conservation gives δJ = (ω/q)δn on each leg exactly, so the eigencondition is model-independent:

  **exp(i(q₊ − q₋)L) = q₊/q₋.**

For the Galilean legs q± = ω/(±s + v₀) it returns Eq. (2)'s drive term and Eq. (4); for the two-field closures it equals the exact clamp determinant to 3×10⁻¹⁴. In the energy-conserving closure a third, advected (entropy) mode exists; with no heat conduction it is frozen into the flow (spatial growth ∝ e^{|Im ω|x/u}) and needs its own source condition.
