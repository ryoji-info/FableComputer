## 0. Why re-issue the row

Table 7's G1 row is right about the two things that matter — the observable (single-cell per-pulse net gain at ≤ 80 °C) and the pass bar (≥ +2.6 dB, beating the 2.555 dB per-gate loss) — and the record has not moved either. What the record *has* moved is everything around them:

1. **The model target is convention-ambiguous by ~2.3 dB.** 07-17 §8 measured the same cell's gain as 10.16 / 9.66 / 9.22 / 7.80 dB depending on cavity tuning, drive kind, and estimator, and wrote the sharpened single spec this note adopts. A pre-registered gate whose target spans 2.3 dB across unstated conventions cannot adjudicate a ~1 dB question. *(Demonstrated in 07-17; consumed here.)*
2. **The architecture's load-bearing waveform changed.** Every current F = 2 frontier number rests on the duty-0.8, slot-confined, data-gated launch at m = 28–30 slots (67–71 GHz), not on the slot-filling or 3-cycle-burst drives (07-31; the burst falls 0.9–3.0 dB short at every slot per [`notes/2026-07-25-e1-burst-scaling-proviso.md`](2026-07-25-e1-burst-scaling-proviso.md)). G1's existence half is unaffected (it is CW small-signal), but the gate's *report* must include the gated-streaming knee, because that is the quantity the requirement ledger now consumes (08-01 §5).
3. **The requirement side is no longer a fixed corner.** 08-01 shows the promoted G_req ledger's CW/pulse corner spread (7.649 / 7.056 dB at F = 2) is one undocumented constant (`A_op` = 0.0116) read against two A_SAT anchors, recommends retiring that constant as a ledger input, and shows the bias convention alone moves the measured comparison by +1.20 dB of gain on one row — more than G1's entire margin question. Its Part III consequence (ii) is binding here: **fix one bias convention across both legs before any frontier number is quotable.** It also withdrew its own draft's recommendation to drive a bench demo at A_fix — the loaded fixed point is by construction the zero-margin drive, the worst design point, not the best.

## 1. The re-issued row (proposal)

Sub-gate naming (G1a/G1b) is this note's proposal, not Table 7's.

| Gate | Observable | Pass threshold | Model target (convention pinned in §2) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **G1a — existence** (Table 7's G1, conventions pinned) | single-cell small-signal net gain at ≤ 80 °C: **retuned cavity, CW drive, intracavity-peak observable**, measurement drive ≥ 10× below the measured knee (the 10× small-signal criterion is **this note's own pin** — no cited source sets it; it is a different quantity from the ≈ 3.8× *operating* drive of §2 (iv)) | **≥ +2.6 dB** (beats the per-gate loss 2.555 dB — unchanged from Table 7) | **+10.2 dB** expected (07-17 §8's sharpened spec; the printed "+8 dB" is the shipped-configuration streaming reading of the same cell — a mis-tuned-configuration artifact per 07-12's grading, separated by pin (i), not an alternative convention of equal standing) |
| **G1b — knee and gated report** (new deliverable, not a new pass bar) | the drive-amplitude sweep G(A) to ≥ 1 dB compression, in **both** conventions of 07-17 (active-cell-only and gain-ratio), for (i) the CW drive and (ii) the duty-0.8 gated streaming train of 07-31 §2 | none — G1b is a **report**, not a gate; its role is to measure A_SAT and retire the imported `A_op` (08-01 (i)) | CW/pulse knee at source amplitude ≈ **0.76–1.15 %**, −1 dB at intracavity swing ≈ **14 ± 2 %** (07-17); gated-streaming knee ≈ **1.2 %** source amplitude at the released grid (08-01 §5, registered key `knee_gated_duty0p8_streaming_m30_N240` = 0.01203–0.01228, band [0.0114, 0.0128]) |

**What a G1a pass does and does not mean (in-model, stated so the bench cannot oversell it):** +2.6 dB proves existence — gain beats one gate's propagation loss. It does **not** close F = 2 logic: on the **measured** routes the requirement chain sits at 6.5–7.3 dB depending on route and convention (small-signal base 6.515 dB; measured-knee route 6.99–7.13 dB at the released grids, ≈ 7.3 extrapolated, band [6.9, 7.4], `A_op`-dependence disclosed in 08-01 §5). The promoted ledger's assumed CW corner reads **7.649 dB** (07-23 §7a, re-derived in 08-01 §1) and remains the promoted value — 08-01 recommends retiring its `A_op` input but reopens no key — so a lab must not read 7.3 as the ceiling; and 08-01's assessment record adds the caution that with only two knee grids, the continuum requirement can exceed 7.4 if the knee's convergence is slower than first order. The F = 2 comparison belongs to G4 (two cells, one junction — Table 7's own "single most informative outcome"), fed by G1b's measured G(A), not to G1a's pass bar.

## 2. The pinned conventions

Each pin names its source and its dB lever, so a deviation is auditable:

- **(i) Cavity tuning:** retuned (in-situ density trim to the measured resonance), with the zero-drift reference reported alongside. Lever: 1.43 dB (07-17 §8).
- **(ii) Drive kind:** CW for G1a; for G1b additionally the 07-31 §2 gated train — duty 0.8, raised-cosine edges of 2 round trips, slot-confined, data-gated, slots m = 28–30 (67–71 GHz). Lever: 0.87 dB in the linear limit alone (07-17 §8).
- **(iii) Estimator:** intracavity-peak for G1a; the released per-slot-mean streaming estimator (07-17's, reproduced to 5.2×10⁻¹⁴ — summation order — in 08-01 §1) for G1b's gated leg. Lever: up to 1.0 dB (07-17 §8). Report the raw per-slot data so any estimator can be recomputed.
- **(iv) Drive amplitude:** pinned relative to the **measured** knee, and two distinct drives are involved, stated separately so they cannot be conflated: the G1a **measurement** drive (≥ 10× below the knee — §1, this note's pin) and the design **operating** drive (≈ 3.8× below the pulse knee — Table 7, unchanged). A "small-signal" gain taken at the operating drive is not small-signal: it folds ≈ 0.06 dB of compression into G1a (07-17's measured bucket), and a drive 3× hotter than operating erodes 0.5+ dB (07-17 §8). **Not at A_fix** — withdrawn by 08-01: in the clocked, re-launched regime a bench demo exercises, A_fix is the drive at which the composed margin is exactly zero.
- **(v) Bias — the one rule this note adds.** The bench has only one threshold: the one it measures, by approach to self-oscillation, which Part I §9.1 states absorbs boundary-condition imperfection into the measured M_th to first order ("Residual non-ideality appears as a modified increment and is exactly what bench gate G1 measures"). The bench bias is therefore **0.7 × the device's own measured threshold** — the tracked-ratio convention, by construction. *Proposal (in-model, maintainer's/crew's call):* model-side comparison values for every bench target must be quoted at the solver's matching convention — bias 0.7·M_th_num(N) at the row's own grid (0.118260 at N = 240, 0.111123 at N = 480), i.e. 08-01 §5's "ratio" leg — **not** at 0.7·M_th_analytic = 0.102958. This is the convention that keeps the operating point fixed as Δx → 0 (08-01 §5's stated lean, for the same reason) and it is the bench's own procedure. This discharges 08-01's consequence (ii) **for the bench-facing spec only**; the solver-side gain-leg re-adjudication stays open (§4, item 2).
- **(vi) Grid honesty (model side):** every model-side comparison value names its N; extrapolants are in-model and never load-bearing (08-01 §5's grid trend: the gated knee moves 11.2–11.6 % between N = 240 and N = 480, referred to the N = 240 value — 0.01203 → 0.01068 gain-ratio, 0.01235 → 0.01092 active-only).
- **(vii) Pattern and scoring (G1b gated leg):** state PRBS pattern, seed, and scored-slot count; the model-side convention is a 40-slot pattern with the first 4 dropped (07-31 §2, adopted by 08-01).

## 3. What the bench reports (deliverables checklist)

1. **G1a:** small-signal gain in the pinned convention, plus the same quantity re-stated in the other three conventions of 07-17 §8's table, so the record's 2.3 dB ambiguity is closed by data rather than perpetuated.
2. **G1b:** the full G(A) sweep, both observables, CW and gated legs, raw per-slot data attached. From it: A_SAT (both conventions), and the requirement evaluated **without** the imported `A_op` — the measured G(A) makes the compression form unnecessary (08-01 §4's construction, used as a report, not as a drive point).
3. **Measured M_th** (approach to self-oscillation), reported against the specular-clamp kinetic expectation 0.165 [0.154, 0.169] — with Part I §10's own two cautions carried (paraphrased): that figure is **not a bench band** (the bench threshold absorbs contact non-ideality by construction, and the diffuse-contact sweep may move the prediction), and its numerical proximity to the retired coarse-grid artifact 0.169 ([`notes/2026-07-22-mth-numerical-vs-physical-viscosity.md`](2026-07-22-mth-numerical-vs-physical-viscosity.md)) is a coincidence that must not be conflated.
4. **The sub-threshold linewidth ladder** (the pre-G1 rung): the three discriminant signatures of [`notes/2026-07-18-boundary-channel-bench-discriminants.md`](2026-07-18-boundary-channel-bench-discriminants.md), now distilled into the "measurement wanted" protocol ([issue #88](https://github.com/ryoji-info/FableComputer/issues/88)). These are cheaper than G1a, falsifiable in both directions, and de-risk every rung above them.

## 4. Standing falsifiers this spec carries

1. **A measured bench knee at ~1 % intracavity swing falsifies the released solver's nonlinearity outright** (07-17 §8 — the model puts the −1 dB point at ≈ 14 ± 2 % intracavity).
2. **The matched-bias requirement check.** The 2026-08-01 assessment record (appended to that note) contains a measurement the session itself did not run: at the gain leg's own analytic bias, the gated requirement reads 6.83 dB (m = 30, N = 240) versus 7.01 at the ratio bias — ~0.18 dB from the convention choice alone, with the registered band [6.9, 7.4] holding only on the ratio leg. Under this note's §2(v) proposal the ratio leg is the operative one, but the model-side gain tables of 07-31/08-01 §4 are at the analytic bias — so **before any bench comparison sheet is assembled, the gain rows must be re-run at the ratio bias** (a cheap, named WP1 item; one row already exists: 08-01 §0.6's m = 26/N = 240 pair).
3. **G1a below +2.6 dB fails the gate.** No convention rescue is available once §2 is pre-registered — that is the point of pre-registering it.

## 5. Limitations and open items

1. **This is a proposal, not an amendment.** Table 7 lives in the manuscript; adoption is a versioned-revision edit, by the maintainer, per the pipeline. Until then the printed row stands and this note is advisory.
2. **The bias rule is discharged bench-facing only.** The solver-side fork — which bias the gated F = 2 *model adjudication* should use — remains **open** exactly as 08-01 §0.6 left it, and the §4 item 2 rerun is required either way.
3. **08-01 is promoted.** Its promotion PR ([#87](https://github.com/ryoji-info/FableComputer/pull/87), 3-of-3 store vote) was merged by the maintainer while this draft was being written, so all 08-01 citations resolve on `main` — including the appended assessment record that §4 item 2 quotes.
4. **G2–G5 are out of scope and at least G2 is stale.** G2's model target still references the 4-ps-slot ISI expectation (≈3–4 dB p-p), which 07-23 retired for F = 2 logic; the gated record now measures pp 0.04–0.29 dB across 07-31's published duty-0.8 rows (N = 240–720) and 0.43 dB at m = 22/N = 720 (08-01 §6's grading measurement). Re-issuing G2 (and checking G3–G5) is a follow-up this note deliberately does not attempt.
5. **The measurement-design problems are unsolved** (07-18 §8, items 4 and 1): GHz-scale resolution on a ~30 GHz line, µW-class probe SNR, and stable biasing just below self-oscillation (item 4); inhomogeneous broadening across a cell (item 1). This note specifies what to measure, not the instrument.
6. **Relative links were rewritten at promotion.** The draft's `../` links resolved from `notes/drafts/`; this promotion applied the mechanical `drafts/` → `notes/` link rewrite the draft's own Limitation 6 required, per the 07-18 precedent. No other body text changed at promotion.
7. **Everything rides on the experimentally unproven gain cell (bench gate G1).** This note is that gate's specification — the sentence bottoms out here. G1 can fail, and a clean fail under this spec would be one of the most informative results the project can produce.
