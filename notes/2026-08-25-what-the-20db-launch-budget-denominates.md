## 2. The launch-limited decode ceiling, in closed form

*In-model (exact algebra of the released Gaussian rules; every stated point re-executed).* Let η = η_in·η_fo and S the coded SNR. At the decision plane (digital 2-bit decode): x_range = 2√(η·N_op), half-gap d² = η·N_op/4, launch variance at decision η·2·N_op·10^(−S/10), and V_static = (x_range/k)²/12 = η·N_op/(3k²). All three scale identically in η·N_op, so their ratios are pure numbers:

  V_launch/d² = 8·10^(−S/10)  V_static/d² = 4/(3k²) (= 1/192 at k_dec = 16)

giving the N_op → ∞ ceiling, with the static term's parameter c explicit:

  **P₂bit^∞(S, k, c) = (3/4)·erfc( [2·(8·10^(−S/10) + 16c²/k²)]^(−1/2) )**

*Demonstrated checks:* P^∞(20, 16, 1/√12) = 4.5978×10⁻⁴; released `error_2bit(4, N_op=1 638 400, launch_snr_dB=20)` = 4.6011×10⁻⁴; 300 K → 4.6446×10⁻⁴; j_dB = 3 → 4.6059×10⁻⁴ (max deviation 1.02 %). **As S → ∞ the form reduces to (3/4)·erfc(k/(4√2·c)) — exactly the registered `avg16_floor_c_kdec` form**, reproducing its stored values to 1 ulp on this platform at c = 1/√12 (8.726643×10⁻⁴⁴) and c = 0.2 (4.130436×10⁻⁸⁹).

**Invariances (demonstrated at the asymptote):** invariant in N_op, temperature (0.9 % across 4→300 K), loss and junction quality (η cancels exactly; 0.1 % across j_dB 1→3). **Non-invariances:** at the registered N_op = 400 the wall (3.365×10⁻³) sits 7.3× above the ceiling — pulse energy buys that factor and then nothing. Below the asymptote, with the criterion stated: on the *variance* criterion the launch term is the majority of the decision variance only below 45.7 K (1.970 vs 2.994 at 77 K); on the *error-ratio* criterion it still multiplies the error 6.28× at 77 K and 2.03× at 150 K, crossing 2× at 151.8 K.

**The static term, on 07-15's corrected axis.** Per notes/2026-07-15's §6 erratum — adopted into Part II, which reads verbatim "the static term V_static = c²(x_range/k_dec)² is the comparator's **input-referred trim offset, not the tanh band** (Section 5), with c open and coded at 1/√12 = 0.289" — the composed term is a slot-static random offset (Gaussian defensible per 07-15 §2), so the live caveat is *magnitude*, not category: 07-15 bounds the quantified mechanism at c ≤ 0.02 (rail-clamped) to 0.0065 (unclamped), and at c = 0.02 the ceiling is 3.0585×10⁻⁴ — 0.21 % from the **launch-only ceiling (3/4)·erfc(10^(S/20)/4)** = 3.0521×10⁻⁴ at S = 20, which is therefore also the magnitude-honest centre. One composition disclosure: the tail combines a slot-correlated offset with per-shot terms in one distribution — an ensemble statement over devices/trims, per Part II's own open "whether the trim residual is truly slot-static".
